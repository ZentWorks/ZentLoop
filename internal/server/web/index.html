ZENTLOOP HELP

Quick guide

Trusted Domains

Domains that belong to your own infrastructure. Adding a root domain also covers its subdomains. Trusted domains appear as normal targets and are included in the regular target statistics.

Date & Target filters

LIVE is the rolling last 24 hours and keeps receiving new Web and SSH events. Selecting a day or All dates switches to a historical view: live streams are paused and lazy loading stays inside the selected time window. The Target filter can be combined with any time view and groups trusted subdomains under their configured root domain.

Live View

The LIVE button opens a compact realtime page with active SSH sessions first, active Web sessions second and the SSH activity feed below. Selecting any session opens the same detailed live drawer used by the dashboard. The view reuses the existing SSE streams and is optimized for phones as well as desktop screens.

Untrusted hosts

Hosts shown in parentheses were requested by a visitor but are not trusted as one of your own domains. They are evaluated separately so they do not distort your normal target statistics. If you trust one later, its retained history is reclassified automatically.

Host sweeps

ZentLoop can recognize when one visitor rapidly tries many different host names. These attempts stay in the untrusted-host view and may be guided into a generic Web Trap instead of being treated as real targets.

Integration / Proxy

When ZentLoop is used behind a compatible proxy or upstream integration, the original public target and visitor information can be preserved. Verified, explicit proxy routes may also provide trusted targets automatically.

Web Sessions

Shows activity against the Web Traps, including the requested paths and how far a visitor follows the presented breadcrumbs.

SSH Sessions

Shows SSH login attempts, commands and movement through the virtual SSH environment and its deception paths.

Self Protection

These counters show when ZentLoop protects itself instead of serving unlimited work, for example by shedding excess live viewers, limiting SSH abuse or warning when retained storage grows unusually high.

RiskHow suspicious or aggressive the observed session currently appears.
ActorZentLoop's behavioral classification of the visitor, such as automated or human-like activity.
AutoHow strongly the behavior resembles automation or a bot.
DepthHow far the visitor has moved into the deception flow.
LoopHow often ZentLoop has successfully led the visitor onward through additional deception.
FrustrationSignals from repeated, unsuccessful or increasingly persistent attempts.

ZentLoop reports observed behavior and behavioral assessments. These indicators support investigation, but they are not a definitive identification of a person or attacker.
